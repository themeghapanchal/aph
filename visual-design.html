Animeghaa Production House is a creative studio led by Meghaa Ruparel, with a deep love for print, brand identity, and design that feels both rooted and refined. We work across brand systems, editorial layouts, packaging, illustration, and 3D design, from concept to final artwork.

Our work draws on cultural context, typographic precision, and a strong visual sensibility. We believe good design doesn't just look right. It tells a story that sticks.

We have worked with brands across the arts, hospitality, publishing, and wellness sectors, helping each one find a visual language that is distinctly theirs.
