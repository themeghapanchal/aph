03. About

Made With Intent.

I'm Meghaa Panchal, a graphic designer with a thing for print, brand systems, and the kind of work that earns a second look. I move across brand identity, editorial, packaging, and illustration, from first sketch to final artwork.

My work runs on cultural context, typographic precision, and a stubborn refusal to make anything forgettable. Good design isn't decoration. It's a story you can't stop thinking about.

I've worked with brands across the arts, hospitality, and wellness sectors, helping them sound like themselves and look like no one else.
